About the portal

A secure connection between parents, students and school.

The Mfantsipim School Exeat Portal is designed to make exeat requests more organised and accessible. Parents can sign in, establish a verified connection with their child, and access the relevant services available through the portal.

Sign in to the Portal See How Verification Works

What can you do here?

The portal brings important parent-facing services into one place, with verification intended to help protect student information and exeat requests.

🔐

Parent account verification

Sign in with an eligible Google account and follow the school's process for confirming your identity and eligibility to use the parent portal.

👨‍👩‍👦

Link to your child

Provide the student's admission number and full name so the school can check the student record and review your request to be linked as a parent or guardian.

📋

Exeat services

Once the necessary access and student link have been approved, use the available internal or external exeat services according to school procedures.

How to get verified

Follow these steps to establish your parent portal access. Verification may require action by the school's authorised staff.

STEP 01

Use the correct Google account

Open the Parent Login page and sign in with the Google account registered or approved for use with the school. Signing in proves access to that account; it does not by itself verify that you are a student's parent.

STEP 02

Check your invitation

Follow the school's parent invitation process if you have been invited. If you have not received an invitation or cannot sign in, contact the school through its official channels for assistance.

STEP 03

Provide accurate parent details

Complete the parent information requested by the portal. Make sure the details you submit are accurate and consistent with the school's official records.

STEP 04

Wait for the school's review

School staff may check your information against authorised records and contact you using the phone number held by the school to confirm your identity and relationship to the student.

STEP 05

Receive a verification decision

Follow any instructions given by the school. If more information is required or verification is not approved, contact the school for clarification rather than submitting conflicting details.

STEP 06

Open your parent dashboard

After your account is approved and the relevant access has been enabled, sign in and check your dashboard for the next steps.

Important: Never share your Google password or verification codes with anyone claiming to be school staff. Use the school's official contact channels if you need help.

How to get linked to your child

Account verification and child linking are separate checks. A verified parent account should only access a student after the parent–student relationship has been reviewed and approved.

STEP 01

Sign in to your verified account

Open your parent dashboard using the Google account approved for the portal.

STEP 02

Start a child-link request

Use the child-link or add-student option if it is available on your dashboard. If you cannot find it, contact the school for the approved linking procedure.

STEP 03

Enter the student's details

Enter the student's admission number and full name exactly as recorded by the school. Check the details before submitting the request.

STEP 04

Choose your relationship

Select the appropriate relationship, such as parent or guardian, when the form asks for it. Provide only information you are authorised to submit.

STEP 05

Allow the school to verify the link

The student details should be checked against the school's records. Staff may confirm the relationship using the parent contact information held by the school before approving the link.

STEP 06

Check your dashboard

Once the school approves the request, check whether the child appears in your linked-students section. If the details do not match or the request is pending, contact the school for assistance.

Privacy and safety: Knowing a student's admission number and name is not sufficient proof of a parent–student relationship. The school should approve the link before student-specific information or exeat actions become available.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about parent access.

Does Google sign-in verify me as a parent?

No. Google sign-in authenticates your account. The school must separately verify your eligibility and relationship to the student.

What if the student's name is not recognised?

Check the admission number and full name against official school information. If they are correct, contact the school to check the student record.

Can I link more than one child?

Ask the school to confirm the procedure for linking additional children. Each parent–student relationship should be reviewed and approved separately.

What if my request is still pending?

A pending request has not yet been approved. Contact the school through its official channels if you need an update or believe the information is incorrect.

Ready to access the parent portal?

Sign in with your approved account. If you still need verification or a child link, follow the school's process before submitting student-related requests.
