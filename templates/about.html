How to get verified

Follow these steps to establish your parent portal access. School staff may need to review your information before access is approved.

STEP 01

Use the correct Google account

Open the Parent Login page and sign in with the Google account registered or approved for use with the school. Signing in proves access to the account; it does not by itself verify your relationship to a student.

Open Parent Login →

STEP 02

Check your invitation

Follow the school's parent invitation process if you have been invited. If you have not received an invitation or cannot sign in, contact the school through its official channels.

STEP 03

Provide accurate parent details

Complete the parent information requested by the portal. Ensure the details you submit are accurate and consistent with the school's official records.

Continue to child linking →

STEP 04

Wait for the school's review

Authorised school staff may check your information against school records and contact you using the parent phone number held by the school.

STEP 05

Receive a verification decision

Follow any instructions provided by the school. If further information is required, contact the school for clarification.

STEP 06

Open your parent dashboard

Once your account has been approved and access enabled, visit your dashboard to check your status and next steps.

{% if user.is_authenticated %} Open Parent Dashboard → {% else %} Sign in to continue → {% endif %}

Important: Never share your Google password or verification codes with anyone claiming to be school staff. Use the school's official contact channels if you need assistance.

How to get linked to your child

Account verification and child linking are separate checks. Student-specific information and exeat actions should only become available after the school approves the relationship.

STEP 01

Sign in to your account

Sign in with the Google account approved for the parent portal.

{% if not user.is_authenticated %}

Parent Login →

{% endif %}
STEP 02

Start a child-link request

Use the child-link or add-student option if it is available on your dashboard. If you cannot find it, contact the school for the approved procedure.

{% if user.is_authenticated %}

Open Parent Dashboard →

{% endif %}
STEP 03

Enter the student's details

Enter the student's admission number and full name exactly as recorded by the school. Review the details before submitting your request.

STEP 04

Choose your relationship

Select the appropriate relationship, such as parent or guardian, when requested. Submit only information you are authorised to provide.

STEP 05

Allow the school to verify the link

School staff should check the student record and may confirm the relationship using the parent contact information held by the school.

STEP 06

Check your dashboard

Once approved, check whether the child appears in your linked-students section. If the request is still pending, contact the school for assistance.

Privacy and safety: Knowing a student's admission number and name is not sufficient proof of a parent–student relationship. The school should approve the link before student-specific information or exeat actions become available.

Frequently asked questions

Select a question to read the answer.

Does Google sign-in verify me as a parent?

No. Google sign-in authenticates your account. The school must separately verify your eligibility and relationship to the student.

Read the verification guide →

What if the student's name is not recognised?

Check the admission number and full name against official school information. If the details are correct, contact the school to check the student record.

Read the child-linking guide →

Can I link more than one child?

Ask the school to confirm the procedure for linking additional children. Each parent–student relationship should be reviewed and approved separately.

Learn about child linking →

What if my request is still pending?

A pending request has not yet been approved. Contact the school through its official channels if you need an update or believe the information is incorrect.

Where can I find school announcements?

Open the announcements page to read published school notices and updates.

Visit Announcements →
