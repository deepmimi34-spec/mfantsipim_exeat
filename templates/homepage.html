About the Exeat Portal

The Mfantsipim School Digital Exeat Portal is designed to make selected parent and exeat processes easier to access online.

Student information and parent relationships must be checked against school records. Submitting an online request does not itself constitute school approval.

Parents should provide accurate information and follow the school's verification procedures.

Protect Your Information

Enter only the student information requested by the portal. Never share your Google password. If the student details do not match school records, contact the school through its established communication channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Open a question to read the answer.

How do I link my child?

Sign in if required, open the Link My Child form, and enter the student's admission number and full name. The school must verify your request before the link is approved.

What if the student's details are rejected?

Check that the admission number and full name match the school's records. If they are correct and the problem continues, contact the school for assistance.

Does submitting the form link my child immediately?

No. The form is a request for verification. The relationship should remain pending until the school completes its review and approves it.

Where can I find school announcements?

Use the Announcements link in the main navigation to view published school notices.

What if I cannot sign in?

Confirm that you are using the Google account accepted by the school. If you still cannot access the portal, contact the school for help.
