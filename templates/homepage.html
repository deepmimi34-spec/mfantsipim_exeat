How the Exeat Process Works

The portal supports the school's exeat process; submitting a request does not automatically grant permission to leave.

1

Select a Service

Open the internal or external exeat section, depending on the type of request required.

2

Submit the Request

Complete the relevant request form and provide accurate information for school review.

3

Await Authorisation

Follow the school's verification and approval procedures before making arrangements to leave.

Important: Students must follow school rules and obtain the required authorisation before leaving school grounds.
